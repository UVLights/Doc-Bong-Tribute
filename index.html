In loving memory of

Hilbart S. Pizarro, DVM

April 1, 1966  –  April 27, 2025

A great husband, father, grandfather, brother, uncle and friend.

A life spent caring

Known to many as Doc Bong, Hilbart was orphaned at sixteen. He built a life anyway — working his way through school and earning his degree in veterinary medicine, then spending his career caring for animals who couldn't tell him what hurt.

A loving husband

He and his wife were partners in every sense of the word. Through life's ups and downs, their bond stayed steady — proof, his family says, of what love and commitment can hold.

A selfless father

He gave without hesitation, working hard so his family never went without, and showing them what it looks like to be a steady, loving presence.

A compassionate veterinarian

He met every animal that came to him with the same patience and care. As his family put it: “Veterinarians are not just an animal doctor, but the gentle voice for those who cannot speak.”

“His legacy lives on in the memories we hold dear, and the lessons he imparted to all who had the privilege of knowing him.” His family
“Well done, good and faithful servant.” Matthew 25:23
